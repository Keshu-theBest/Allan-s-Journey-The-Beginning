THE TABLE RULES · VERSION 1

A little strategy.
A lot of other people.

Pick one number and one suit. Everyone chooses in secret. Your score depends on what the whole table chooses.

  1. Six rounds. One card each.

    Choose a number from 2 to 10 and any of the four suits. Aces and face cards are excluded. You have 30 seconds per round. A locked card cannot change. Each number can be used only once per match; suits can be reused. Multiple players can choose the same card.

  2. Aim below the average.

    For the cards submitted this round, let A be the average number. Your target is:

    T = 2 + ⅔ × (A − 2)

    For your chosen number v, earn:

    Number points = max(0, 60 − 10 × |v − T|)

    The closer you are to the target, the more you earn. Your own card is included in the average. Save useful low cards for later rounds.

  3. Find the common suit.

    The most selected suit earns 24 suit points. Its other suit of the same color earns 12; the opposite color earns 0. Hearts and diamonds are red; clubs and spades are black.

    Tied most popular suits: calculate this bonus against each tied suit, then take the average. There is no random tie-break.

  4. The crowd comes at a cost.

    If n players submitted and c chose your exact suit:

    Crowding penalty = 18 × (c − 1) / (n − 1)

    The penalty is 0 when n ≤ 1. Being in the majority can pay, but a packed suit dilutes that reward.

    Round score = number points + suit points − crowding penalty

    Round scores can be negative. Round the final round score to 2 decimal places. The highest sum after six rounds wins. Equal totals share a rank.

  5. Miss the clock, lose 15.

    No card by the deadline means −15 points. No number is spent, and you are excluded from that round’s average and suit counts. If nobody submits, everyone loses 15. Disconnecting does not pause the match; return in the same browser to reconnect. Leaving forfeits your seat, and missed future rounds cost 15 points each.

ONE ROUND, EXPLAINED

The table plays 10♥, 7♦, 6♦, 5♠. Average: 7. Target: 5⅓. Diamonds are most popular.

Your 10♥ earns 13⅓ number points + 12 suit points − 0 crowding penalty = 25.33 points.

Choices are revealed together when everyone locks in or time runs out. Reveals last 8 seconds before the next round. Games need 2–160 players; practice adds seven bots. This is a game of strategic prediction, with no stakes, betting, or real-money rewards. Rooms are temporary and reset when the server restarts.
